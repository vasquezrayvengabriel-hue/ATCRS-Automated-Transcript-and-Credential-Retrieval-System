Privacy Policy

Learn how information is collected, used, protected, and managed when using the Automated Transcript and Credential Retrieval System.

Home Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

The Automated Transcript and Credential Retrieval System (ATCRS) of De La Salle John Bosco College (DLSJBC) is designed to support secure and efficient academic document and credential services.

This Privacy Policy explains the general way information may be collected, used, stored, protected, and disclosed when authorized users access ATCRS.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the service being used, ATCRS may process information necessary to identify users and complete authorized academic transactions.

  • Student identification information.
  • Full name and other basic profile information.
  • Academic records relevant to requested services.
  • Document request information.
  • Clearance and payment transaction information.
  • Credential verification information.
  • Account and system activity information.

3. Purpose of Information Processing

Information processed through ATCRS may be used for legitimate academic and administrative purposes, including:

  • Managing student accounts.
  • Processing academic document requests.
  • Reviewing clearance requirements.
  • Processing authorized transactions.
  • Releasing approved documents.
  • Verifying academic credentials.
  • Maintaining appropriate institutional records.
  • Protecting the security and integrity of the system.

4. Accuracy of Information

Users are expected to provide accurate, complete, and current information when using ATCRS.

Incorrect or incomplete information may result in delays in processing requests or verification transactions.

5. Data Security

ATCRS is designed with security measures intended to help protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or misuse.

  • Account authentication.
  • Role-based system access.
  • Restricted administrative functions.
  • Credential verification controls.
  • System activity and audit monitoring.
Users should protect their account credentials and immediately report suspicious or unauthorized activity.

6. Role-Based Access

ATCRS uses separate access areas based on the responsibilities of each authorized user role.

Student

Accesses personal academic information and authorized document services.

Registrar

Manages authorized academic records, requests, releases, and administrative functions.

Verifier

Performs authorized credential verification activities.

7. Information Sharing

Information processed through ATCRS should only be accessed or disclosed for legitimate institutional, academic, administrative, verification, security, or other authorized purposes.

Access to information is limited according to the user's authorized role and system permissions.

8. Credential Verification

Credential verification information may be processed when an authorized verifier submits a legitimate verification request.

Verification services must not be used to obtain information for unauthorized, fraudulent, or unlawful purposes.

9. Data Retention

Information and transaction records may be retained for as long as necessary to support legitimate academic, administrative, institutional, security, and recordkeeping requirements.

Retention periods may depend on the type of information, the purpose of processing, and applicable institutional requirements.

10. Technical and Session Information

ATCRS may use technical information and temporary session information to support authentication, navigation, system security, and proper operation of the portal.

Session information may be used to maintain a user's authenticated state while accessing protected areas of the system.

11. User Responsibilities

  • Keep account credentials confidential.
  • Do not access another person's account.
  • Do not attempt to bypass system security controls.
  • Do not modify or falsify academic information.
  • Log out after completing a session, especially when using a shared device.
  • Report suspected security incidents to the appropriate DLSJBC office.

12. Third-Party Services

Certain technical services or external resources may be used to support the operation of the system.

Where applicable, such services may have their own privacy policies and terms that govern their handling of information.

13. Privacy and Information Rights

Users may raise questions or concerns regarding the handling of their information through the appropriate DLSJBC office or authorized system administrator.

Requests concerning personal information may be subject to identity verification, institutional procedures, and applicable requirements.

14. Security Incidents

Suspected unauthorized access, account compromise, data exposure, or other security incidents should be reported promptly to the appropriate DLSJBC office or system administrator.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes in ATCRS, institutional policies, security practices, or applicable requirements.

Updated versions will replace previous versions when published through the appropriate ATCRS or DLSJBC channels.

16. Contact and Support

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of information within ATCRS, please contact the appropriate DLSJBC office or authorized system administrator.

17. Acknowledgment

By using ATCRS, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and understand that information may be processed as necessary to provide authorized ATCRS services.
