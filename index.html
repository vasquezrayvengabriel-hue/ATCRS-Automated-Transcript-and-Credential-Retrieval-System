ABOUT ATCRS

A smarter way to manage academic records.

The Automated Transcript and Credential Retrieval System is designed to streamline the collection, processing, generation, and retrieval of academic records and credentials.

👨‍🎓

For Alumni

Request transcripts, diplomas, certificates, and other academic credentials online.

🏫

For Registrar

Manage requests, validate clearance, process payments, and release certified academic records.

🔎

For Verifiers

Securely verify academic credentials for employment, admissions, and other legitimate purposes.

SYSTEM FEATURES

Everything needed for digital credential processing.

01

Online Requests

Alumni can submit academic record requests from anywhere using the ATCRS portal.

02

Clearance Validation

Registrar staff can review and validate student clearance before document release.

03

Online payment status can be monitored and confirmed during request processing.

04
