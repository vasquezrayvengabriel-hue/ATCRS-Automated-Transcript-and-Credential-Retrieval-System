OUR SERVICES

Academic Credential Services

ATCRS provides a convenient digital process for requesting and managing academic credentials.

Request Documents

Submit requests for transcripts, diplomas, certificates, and other authorized academic credentials.

Track Requests

Monitor the progress and current status of your document requests through your account.

Online Payment

View applicable document fees and payment status through the digital request process.

Credential Verification

Verify the authenticity of released academic credentials using the appropriate credential information.

HOW IT WORKS

Simple and Organized Process

From submitting a request to receiving your credential, ATCRS keeps the process organized and easy to follow.

01

Create an Account

Applicants, students, and alumni create an authorized ATCRS account to access document services.

02

Submit Request

Select the academic document you need and provide the required information and purpose.

03

Verification & Processing

Authorized DLSJBC personnel review the request, records, clearance, and applicable requirements.

04

Receive Credential

Once approved and released, the available credential can be retrieved through the user's account.
