Home Terms of Use

1. Introduction

These Terms of Use govern your access to and use of the Automated Transcript and Credential Retrieval System (ATCRS) of De La Salle John Bosco College (DLSJBC).

By accessing or using ATCRS, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to comply with these Terms of Use and all applicable institutional policies.

2. Acceptable Use

ATCRS is provided to authorized students, registrars, administrators, and credential verifiers for legitimate academic and institutional purposes.

  • Use the system only for authorized academic or credential-related transactions.
  • Provide accurate and truthful information when submitting requests.
  • Keep your login credentials secure.
  • Use only the account assigned to you.
  • Follow all instructions and procedures provided by DLSJBC.

3. Account Security

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account information and passwords.

You must immediately report suspected unauthorized access, compromised credentials, or suspicious activity to the appropriate DLSJBC office or system administrator.

Never share your password or allow another person to use your account.

4. Student Information

Students must provide complete and accurate information when using ATCRS services.

Information submitted through the system may be used to process academic records, document requests, credential verification, and other authorized institutional services.

5. Document Requests

ATCRS may allow authorized users to request academic and institutional documents, subject to applicable DLSJBC policies, clearance requirements, and payment requirements.

Submission of a request does not guarantee immediate release of a document. Requests may require review, verification, clearance, processing, and approval before release.

6. Credential Verification

Credential verification services are intended only for legitimate verification of academic documents and credentials issued or recorded by DLSJBC.

Verifiers must not use verification information for unauthorized purposes, fraudulent activities, harassment, or unlawful discrimination.

7. Prohibited Activities

Users are prohibited from attempting to:

  • Access another user's account without authorization.
  • Alter, manipulate, or falsify academic records or credentials.
  • Bypass authentication or security controls.
  • Introduce malicious software or harmful code into the system.
  • Attempt unauthorized access to system databases or administrative functions.
  • Use the system for fraudulent or unlawful activities.

8. System Availability

DLSJBC may temporarily suspend or restrict access to ATCRS for maintenance, upgrades, security measures, technical issues, or other legitimate institutional reasons.

The College will make reasonable efforts to maintain system availability and restore services when interruptions occur.

9. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for reviewing the information they submit and ensuring that requests and transactions are accurate.

Users may be required to provide additional information or documentation when necessary to validate an academic transaction.

10. Privacy

The collection and processing of information through ATCRS are subject to the system's Privacy Policy and applicable institutional privacy requirements.

Please review our Privacy Policy for additional information about how information may be handled.

11. Suspension or Termination

DLSJBC reserves the right to suspend, restrict, or terminate access to ATCRS when necessary, including in cases involving unauthorized use, security violations, fraudulent activity, or violation of these Terms of Use.

12. Changes to These Terms

DLSJBC may update or modify these Terms of Use when necessary to reflect changes in institutional policies, system functions, security requirements, or applicable regulations.

Continued use of ATCRS after updated terms are published may constitute acceptance of the revised Terms of Use.

13. Contact and Support

If you have questions regarding these Terms of Use or need assistance with ATCRS, please contact the appropriate DLSJBC office or authorized system administrator.

14. Acceptance of Terms

By using ATCRS, you confirm that you have read and understood these Terms of Use and agree to comply with them.
