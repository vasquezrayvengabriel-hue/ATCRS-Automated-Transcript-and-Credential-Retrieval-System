Clearance Workflow

Clearance must be completed before document processing continues.

1
Student Verification

Confirm the student's record and identity.

↓
2
Clearance Review

Check whether the student has pending academic or administrative obligations.

↓
3
Payment

Once cleared, the request may proceed to the payment stage.

↓
4
Processing

The Registrar may continue processing the document request.

Clearance Status Guide

Pending

Clearance has not yet been reviewed.

Under Review

The Registrar is checking the student's clearance.

Cleared

The student is cleared and the request may continue.

Not Cleared

The request cannot continue until the clearance issue is resolved.

🔐

Clearance Security

Clearance information is restricted to authorized Registrar personnel. Students and public users cannot directly modify their clearance status.
