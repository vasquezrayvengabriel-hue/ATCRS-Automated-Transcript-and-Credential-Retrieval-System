Privacy Notice

Protection of personal and academic information

Information We Collect

The ATCRS prototype may collect information such as Student ID, full name, email address, account type, and information associated with academic record requests.

Purpose of Collection

Information is used to demonstrate account management, academic record requests, document processing, and credential verification workflows.

Data Protection

Personal and academic information should only be accessed by authorized users and should be protected against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

Third-Party Verification

Verification requests should only provide information necessary for legitimate credential verification and should follow applicable privacy requirements.

Prototype Limitation

This demonstration stores selected prototype information locally in the browser. It should not be used to store real sensitive academic records or confidential credentials.

User Responsibility

Users should provide accurate information and must not attempt to access another person's academic records without proper authorization.
